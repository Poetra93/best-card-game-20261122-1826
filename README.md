**Sigil Duel** is a complete original duel card game: you face Vex, a rival
duelist, across a green table. You both hold 30 life. Each turn you draw,
gain 3 ember, and play sigil cards from your hand — 24 original cards across
four suits in a beats-cycle (Flame beats Gale, Gale beats Tide, Tide beats
Stone, Stone beats Flame), each suit told apart by its own colour and drawn
glyph. Chain cards of one suit for bonus power, and counter Vex's telegraphed
suit — Vex shows you the suit it is charging, power hidden — for +3 damage.
Some cards draw, heal, or ward. Reduce Vex to 0 life to win; fall to 0 and
Vex takes the duel. Mouse and keyboard play, synthesised WebAudio sounds,
and deterministic `?shot=` states for screenshots. No network requests, no
build step: plain static files under `game/`.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

Play: click a card (or press 1–7) to play it, E to end your turn, M to mute,
Enter to start or duel again.
